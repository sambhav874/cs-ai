CHANGE ORDER NO. 1

Change Order No. 1 to Statement of Work SOW-001, issued under the Master
Service Agreement between Harborview Health Systems and Trellis Data
Partners, Inc. dated 3 March 2026.

Dated: 14 July 2026

1. BACKGROUND. Following favorable interim results at the three pilot
facilities under SOW-001, Harborview Health Systems has requested expansion
of the pilot prior to network-wide rollout.

2. CHANGES TO SOW-001.
(a) The pilot scope is expanded from three (3) to five (5) facilities.
(b) The implementation timeline is extended from sixteen (16) to twenty
(20) weeks to accommodate onboarding of the two additional facilities.
(c) The implementation fee under Exhibit B is increased by USD 95,000,
calculated on the same per-facility rate basis as the original three
facilities, payable upon go-live at the two additional facilities.

3. SERVICE LEVELS UNCHANGED. The service levels set out in Exhibit C (SLA
Exhibit) remain unchanged and shall apply to all five (5) facilities upon
their respective go-live dates.

4. NO OTHER CHANGES. All other terms of SOW-001 remain unchanged.
