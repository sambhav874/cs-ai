MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 3 March
2026 ("Effective Date") between HARBORVIEW HEALTH SYSTEMS ("Client") and
TRELLIS DATA PARTNERS, INC. ("Vendor"). Confidential information exchanged
by the Parties in connection with this Agreement remains governed by the
Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between the Parties dated 12 January 2026.

1. TERM. This Agreement continues for two (2) years from the Effective Date
unless earlier terminated as provided herein.

2. SERVICES. Vendor shall perform Services pursuant to one or more
Statements of Work or Order Forms executed by both Parties, each referencing
this Agreement by date.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. Client owns all work product created specifically
for Client under a Statement of Work. Vendor retains ownership of its
pre-existing analytics platform and underlying models.

4. LIABILITY. Each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall
not exceed USD 3,000,000, except for breaches of confidentiality or data
protection obligations, which are uncapped.

5. INSURANCE. Vendor shall maintain, at its own expense: (a) Commercial
General Liability insurance of not less than USD 2,000,000 per occurrence;
(b) Professional Liability/Errors & Omissions insurance of not less than
USD 5,000,000 in the aggregate; and (c) Cyber Liability insurance of not
less than USD 5,000,000 in the aggregate. Client shall be named as
additional insured, evidenced by a certificate of insurance provided to
Client prior to commencement of Services.

6. TERMINATION. Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience
upon ninety (90) days' written notice, or for material breach upon thirty
(30) days' written notice if the breach remains uncured. Upon termination,
Vendor shall provide reasonable transition assistance and comply with data
return/deletion obligations under any Data Processing Addendum in effect.

7. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
Ohio.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the
Effective Date.
