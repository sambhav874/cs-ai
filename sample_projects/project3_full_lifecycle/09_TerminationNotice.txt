NOTICE OF TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE

Date: 10 November 2026

To: Trellis Data Partners, Inc.
Attn: K. Ostrander, Chief Revenue Officer

From: Harborview Health Systems

Re: Termination of Statement of Work SOW-001 under the Master Service
Agreement dated 3 March 2026

Dear Ms. Ostrander,

This letter serves as formal notice of termination for convenience,
invoking the termination-for-convenience clause of the Master Service
Agreement between Harborview Health Systems and Trellis Data Partners, Inc.
dated 3 March 2026 (the "Agreement").

Pursuant to the Agreement, this notice terminates Statement of Work SOW-001
(as modified by Change Order No. 1 dated 14 July 2026) effective ninety
(90) days from the date of this notice, i.e., 8 February 2027.

This decision follows Harborview's internal review and resolution to
consolidate its analytics vendor relationships onto a single platform, and
is not related to any performance concern with Trellis's delivery under
SOW-001.

We request that Trellis provide a transition/wind-down plan consistent with
the exit-assistance provisions of the Agreement, and confirm that all data
return and deletion obligations under the Data Processing Addendum dated 24
March 2026 will be fulfilled within the timeframes specified therein.

We appreciate Trellis's partnership during this engagement and look forward
to an orderly transition.

Sincerely,

L. Fennimore
VP Strategic Partnerships
Harborview Health Systems
