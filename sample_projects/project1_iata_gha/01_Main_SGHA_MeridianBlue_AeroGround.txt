STANDARD GROUND HANDLING AGREEMENT

This Standard Ground Handling Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
1 June 2026 ("Effective Date") between:

MERIDIAN BLUE AIRWAYS, a company incorporated under the laws of Ireland,
having its registered office at 14 Airport Business Park, Dublin, Ireland
("Airline"),

and

AEROGROUND SERVICES LTD., a company incorporated under the laws of Germany,
having its registered office at Cargo City Süd, 60549 Frankfurt am Main,
Germany ("Handler"),

each a "Party" and together the "Parties," for the provision of ground
handling services at Frankfurt Airport (FRA) ("Station").

1. TERM. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for
an initial term of three (3) years, automatically renewing for successive
one (1) year terms unless either Party gives the other not less than ninety
(90) days' written notice of non-renewal prior to the end of the then-current
term.

2. SCOPE AND CHARGES. The Handler shall perform Ground Handling Services at
the Station strictly in accordance with Annex A (Standard Ground Handling
Services) and shall invoice the Airline at the rates set out in Annex B
(Charges), each of which is incorporated herein by reference and attached as
a schedule to this Agreement. In the event of any conflict between the body
of this Agreement and Annex A or Annex B, this Agreement shall prevail as to
commercial terms and Annex A/B shall prevail as to operational and pricing
detail within their respective scope.

3. LIABILITY AND INSURANCE. The Handler's aggregate liability to the
Airline under this Agreement shall not exceed USD 2,500,000 per incident,
and in no event shall either Party be liable for indirect, incidental, or
consequential damages. The Handler shall maintain aviation liability
insurance with a combined single limit of not less than USD 50,000,000,
naming the Airline as additional insured, and shall provide evidence of such
coverage upon request.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION. This Agreement is governed by the
laws of the Federal Republic of Germany. Any dispute arising under this
Agreement that cannot be resolved by good-faith negotiation within thirty
(30) days shall be referred to arbitration under the IATA Ground Handling
Agreement arbitration procedures, seated in Geneva, Switzerland.

5. TERMINATION. Either Party may terminate this Agreement for material
breach upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the breach remains uncured
at the end of such period, or for convenience upon one hundred eighty (180)
days' written notice to the other Party.

6. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement, together with Annex A and Annex B and
any amendments executed by both Parties, constitutes the entire agreement
between the Parties concerning ground handling services at the Station.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the
Effective Date.
