ANNEX A — STANDARD GROUND HANDLING SERVICES

Annex A to the AeroGround Services Ltd. / Meridian Blue Airways Ground
Handling Agreement dated 1 June 2026 (Frankfurt Airport, FRA Station).

This Annex A sets out the scope of Ground Handling Services to be performed
by the Handler for the Airline's scheduled operations at the Station, and is
incorporated by reference into the Agreement referenced above. Charges for
the services described below are set out in Annex B (Charges) to the same
Agreement.

SECTION 1 — REPRESENTATION AND ADMINISTRATION
The Handler shall provide flight-file preparation, NOTAM/weather briefing
distribution, and general administrative representation of the Airline at
the Station during all scheduled operating hours.

SECTION 2 — LOAD CONTROL, COMMUNICATIONS AND FLIGHT OPERATIONS
Preparation of load sheets, weight-and-balance documentation, and
departure-control-system (DCS) support for all scheduled flights.

SECTION 3 — PASSENGER AND BAGGAGE SERVICES
Check-in, gate, and arrivals staffing per the following minimum manning
table for each A320/A321-class turnaround: six (6) check-in agents, two (2)
gate agents, and three (3) arrivals staff. Baggage handling, sorting, and
reconciliation per IATA Baggage Reference Manual standards.

SECTION 4 — CARGO AND MAIL
Acceptance, documentation, and ramp transfer of cargo and mail per
applicable customs and IATA Cargo Handling Manual requirements.

SECTION 5 — RAMP SERVICES
Marshalling, ground power unit (GPU) provision, pushback, and de-icing
services (de-icing charges per Annex B). Ramp return handling with load
change is billed as a full turnaround; ramp return without load change is
billed at the technical-landing rate per Annex B.

SECTION 6 — SUPPORT SERVICES
Cabin cleaning (standard turnaround clean and periodic deep clean), potable
water service, and lavatory servicing.

SECTION 7 — SECURITY
Aircraft security searches and access-control support per Station security
program requirements.

SECTION 8 — AIRCRAFT MAINTENANCE LIAISON
Line-maintenance liaison and coordination only; no heavy maintenance checks
are within the scope of this Annex.

Each Section above corresponds to the Annex B rate line item covering the
same scope; charges for cargo/mail handling (Section 4) are invoiced
separately per the applicable cargo rate schedule maintained by the Handler
and are not included in the standard turnaround rate.
