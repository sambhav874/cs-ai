ANNEX B — CHARGES

Annex B to the AeroGround Services Ltd. / Meridian Blue Airways Ground
Handling Agreement dated 1 June 2026 (Frankfurt Airport, FRA Station).

This Annex B sets out the charges payable by the Airline for the Ground
Handling Services described in Annex A to the Agreement referenced above.

P.1 — STANDARD TURNAROUND RATES
P.1.1 A320-family aircraft: EUR 1,850.00 per turnaround. Covers Annex A
Sections 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, and 7.
P.1.2 A321-family aircraft: EUR 2,050.00 per turnaround. Covers Annex A
Sections 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, and 7.

P.2 — CONDITIONAL AND SPECIAL CHARGES
P.2.1 Technical landing / ramp return with no load change: fifty percent
(50%) of the standard turnaround rate applicable to the aircraft type,
reflecting that only Annex A Section 5 (Ramp Services) applies.
P.2.2 De-icing: EUR 320.00 per application (Type I fluid included); Type II
and Type IV fluid are passed through to the Airline at the Handler's actual
cost plus five percent (5%) administration fee.
P.2.3 Cargo and mail handling: per the Handler's standard cargo rate
schedule, invoiced separately from the turnaround rate.
P.2.4 No night, Sunday, or public-holiday surcharge shall apply to any
turnaround operated within the Airline's contracted published flight
schedule as filed with the Station slot coordinator.

P.3 — ANNUAL RATE ESCALATION
Effective each anniversary of the Effective Date (1 June), the rates in
Section P.1 shall escalate by the greater of (a) the annual Eurozone HICP
inflation figure for the preceding twelve (12) months, or (b) two and
one-half percent (2.5%) (the "Escalation Floor").

P.4 — PAYMENT TERMS
Invoices are issued monthly in arrears and are payable within thirty (30)
days of the invoice date (net-30).
