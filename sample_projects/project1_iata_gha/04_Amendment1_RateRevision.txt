AMENDMENT NO. 1

Amendment No. 1 to the AeroGround Services Ltd. / Meridian Blue Airways
Ground Handling Agreement dated 1 June 2026, amending Annex B (Charges).

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") is entered into as of 15 August 2026
between AeroGround Services Ltd. ("Handler") and Meridian Blue Airways
("Airline") (together, the "Parties").

WHEREAS the Parties entered into a Standard Ground Handling Agreement dated
1 June 2026 (the "Agreement"), including Annex A (Standard Ground Handling
Services) and Annex B (Charges) attached thereto; and

WHEREAS the Parties wish to revise certain rates in Annex B outside the
normal annual escalation cycle described in Annex B Section P.3, due to
fuel and labor cost pressures at the Station;

NOW THEREFORE the Parties agree as follows:

1. RATE REVISION. Effective 1 September 2026, the A321-family turnaround
rate in Annex B Section P.1.2 is revised from EUR 2,050.00 to EUR 2,140.00
per turnaround. The A320-family rate in Section P.1.1 is unchanged.

2. NEW DE-ICING TIER. Annex B Section P.2.2 is amended to add a new
pass-through tier for Type II fluid at the Handler's actual cost plus five
percent (5%) administration fee, in addition to the existing Type IV
pass-through terms.

3. NO OTHER CHANGES. Except as expressly amended herein, all terms of the
Agreement, Annex A, and Annex B remain unchanged and in full force and
effect.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Amendment No. 1 as of the
date first written above.
