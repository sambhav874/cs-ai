STATEMENT OF WORK SOW-002

Statement of Work SOW-002, issued under the Master Service Agreement between
Solstice Retail Group, Inc. and Cobalt Digital Solutions, LLC dated 2 March
2026.

1. SCOPE. Ongoing managed support and platform maintenance retainer for
Solstice's legacy point-of-sale (POS) systems, following Solstice's decision
to transition existing legacy POS support to Vendor. This engagement is
independent of, and unrelated in scope to, the inventory-forecasting
implementation described in Statement of Work SOW-001.

2. TERM. Twelve (12) months, commencing 14 July 2026, renewing month-to-month
thereafter unless either Party gives thirty (30) days' notice of
non-renewal.

3. FEES. Monthly retainer of USD 18,500, covering up to forty (40) support
hours per month. Work exceeding the included hours is billed time-and-
materials at the following rates: Senior Engineer USD 210/hour; Support
Analyst USD 130/hour.

4. SERVICE LEVELS.
- Priority 1 (P1) incidents: acknowledged within thirty (30) minutes,
  resolved or workaround provided within four (4) hours.
- Monthly platform uptime target: 99.5%. For each 0.1% shortfall below the
  target in a given month, Client receives a service credit equal to two
  percent (2%) of that month's retainer fee, capped at twenty percent (20%)
  of the monthly fee.

5. PROJECT TEAM.
Solstice IT Manager: D. Okafor
Cobalt Support Lead: P. Ilić

This Statement of Work is governed by the terms of the Master Service
Agreement referenced above.
