## ARTICLE I: DEFINITIONS AND cGMP COMPLIANCE RULES

### Section 1.01: Rules of Interpretation
(a) Terms defined in FDA 21 CFR Parts 210/211, ICH guidelines, or EudraLex Volume 4 shall carry their statutory cGMP meanings unless explicitly modified herein.
(b) All references to days mean calendar days unless specified as business days.

### Section 1.02: Defined Terms
As used in this Agreement, the following defined terms shall have the specified meanings:

**1. Acceptance Criteria:** Numerical limits, ranges, or other suitable measures for testing the safety, identity, strength, quality, and purity of Biopharmaceutical Drug Products.

**2. Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API):** Any substance or mixture of substances intended to be used in the manufacture of a drug product that becomes an active ingredient of the drug product.

**3. Batch Right-First-Time (RFT):** The percentage of completed commercial manufacturing lots that meet all batch release criteria and quality specifications without incurring critical deviations or requiring re-processing.

**4. Bulk Drug Substance (BDS):** Unformulated active biopharmaceutical material produced by cell culture harvest and purification prior to final formulation and filling.

**5. Cleanroom ISO Class 5 Uptime:** The percentage of operational hours during a billing month that critical filling suites (Grade A laminar flow hoods) maintain ISO 5 airborne particle count compliance (< 3,520 particles/m³ >= 0.5 µm).

**6. Critical Quality Attribute (CQA):** A physical, chemical, biological, or microbiological property or characteristic that should be within an appropriate limit, range, or distribution to ensure desired product quality.

**7. Deviation Lead Time:** The number of calendar days elapsed between the initial logging of an out-of-trend event or process parameter drift and the formal approval of the investigation report by CDMO Quality Assurance.

**8. Excursion Event:** Any temperature, humidity, or differential pressure reading outside validated operating ranges lasting longer than five (5) consecutive minutes in any stability chamber, cold storage room, or bioreactor suite.

**9. Failure to Supply:** Any inability of CDMO to deliver at least ninety percent (90%) of a confirmed purchase order lot volume within ten (10) calendar days of the agreed Delivery Deadline.

**10. Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP):** Current Good Manufacturing Practices enforced by the FDA under 21 CFR Parts 210 and 211, and EMA EudraLex Volume 4.

**11. Master Batch Record (MBR):** Document containing the master manufacturing instructions, bill of materials, sampling plans, and quality controls for a commercial lot.

**12. On-Time In-Full (OTIF) Delivery:** The percentage of commercial drug product lots delivered to Sponsor's designated distribution hubs on or before the agreed delivery date with all required certificate of analysis (CoA) documentation.

**13. Out-of-Specification (OOS) Result:** Any analytical test result that fails to meet pre-established specification limits documented in approved regulatory filings.

**14. Process Analytical Technology (PAT):** Systems for designing, analyzing, and controlling manufacturing through timely measurements of critical quality parameters.

**15. Process Yield Efficiency:** The percentage ratio of actual purified protein yield achieved in a commercial bioreactor run to the theoretical maximum yield validated during process characterization.

**16. Quality Agreement:** The detailed regulatory agreement executed concurrently herewith defining specific cGMP responsibilities between Sponsor and CDMO.

**17. Stability Testing Chamber:** Temperature and humidity controlled environmental chambers maintained under ICH Q1A(R2) conditions for long-term real-time stability monitoring.

**18. Master Cell Bank (MCB):** Preserved biological cell repository used as the starting material for all commercial fermentation runs.

**19. Validation Master Plan (VMP):** Summary document detailing the validation strategy, cleaning validation, process performance qualification (PPQ), and facility qualification.

**20. Certificate of Analysis (CoA):** Formal document signed by CDMO Quality Assurance certifying that a specific batch meets all tested specifications.

## ARTICLE II: SCOPE OF BIOMANUFACTURING SERVICES

### Section 2.01: Commercial Manufacturing Operations
CDMO shall manufacture, test, store, and package Sponsor's proprietary biologic drug substance and sterile injectable drug product at the RTP Campus. Services include:
(a) Operation of dual 2,000-Liter single-use bioreactor trains for cell culture harvest;
(b) Downstream purification including Protein A affinity chromatography, viral inactivation, tangential flow filtration (TFF), and bulk formulation;
(c) Aseptic vial fill-finish operating at speeds up to 120 vials per minute under ISO 5 conditions;
(d) Quality control release testing, sterility testing, mycoplasma testing, viral clearance testing, and stability storage.

## ARTICLE 5: OPERATIONAL GOVERNANCE, AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE - SECTION 1


### Section 5.01: Detailed Operational Protocols in Biopharmaceutical CDMO Manufacturing

CDMO shall operate and manage all infrastructure, equipment, processes, software tools, and personnel engaged in Biopharmaceutical CDMO Manufacturing in accordance with established industry standards, strict safety protocols, and statutory requirements. All operational workflows shall be documented in standard operating procedures (SOPs) approved in writing by Sponsor prior to commercial execution.

### Section 5.02: Preventive Maintenance and Quality Control Verification

CDMO shall maintain a comprehensive preventive maintenance and quality control regime. Equipment calibrations, diagnostic sweeps, software patches, and environmental audits shall occur on a mandatory recurring schedule. Detailed maintenance logs shall be preserved in tamper-evident electronic format for at least ten (10) years and made available for Sponsor inspection upon request.

### Section 5.03: Regulatory Inspections, Statutory Reporting, and Audit Rights

Sponsor and its authorized independent auditors shall have unrestricted rights to inspect, audit, test, and evaluate CDMO's facilities, records, telemetry streams, and operating procedures upon five (5) business days written notice. In the event of an unannounced inspection by a federal or state regulatory authority (e.g., FDA, FCC, SEC, EPA, OSHA), CDMO shall notify Sponsor within two (2) hours of inspector arrival.

### Section 5.04: Incident Escalation, Root Cause Analysis (RCA), and Corrective Action Plans

In the event of any operational breakdown, performance failure, security incident, or SLA breach, CDMO shall initiate immediate emergency response protocols. CDMO shall provide a preliminary incident notification within fifteen (15) minutes, contain the issue within thirty (30) minutes, and submit a comprehensive Root Cause Analysis (RCA) report within forty-eight (48) hours detailing permanent Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA).

### Section 5.05: Subcontractor Oversight and Vendor Qualification Rules

CDMO shall not subcontract, delegate, or assign any primary operational duties under this Agreement without Sponsor's express prior written approval. All approved subcontractors must be bound by written agreements enforcing quality control, non-disclosure, cybersecurity, and insurance standards at least as stringent as those set forth herein. CDMO remains fully liable for all subcontractor acts and omissions.

### Section 5.06: Environmental Sustainability, Energy Efficiency, and Waste Disposal

CDMO shall execute all operational activities in compliance with environmental protection regulations. CDMO shall implement energy-efficient practices, minimize hazardous waste generation, and provide certified waste disposition documentation to Sponsor annually.
