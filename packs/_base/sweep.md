# Where obligations hide — family-independent

These are structural, not domain, patterns. They hold for any commercial agreement.

- **A band table's consequence column is a second obligation stream.** Rows map performance to a
  credit *and* to duties — warnings, corrective action plans, executive review. Do not treat the
  column as a label on the row's credit.
- **Read the prose immediately after a table.** Per-unit rates ("per tenth of a percentage point",
  "per event", "per shipment") are frequently stated there rather than in the table itself.
- **A cap lives away from what it caps.** Credit and liability ceilings sit in their own article; a
  credit record that ignores its cap overstates recoverable money.
- **Definitions carry thresholds.** A defined term ("Material Service Failure", "Excursion Event")
  often contains the numeric trigger that an operative clause later relies on.
- **Notice periods attach to rights, not only to breaches.** Renewal, termination for convenience,
  benchmarking and audit rights each carry their own notice window.
- **A duty with no number is still a duty.** Prohibitions ("may not bill unless…"), evidence
  preconditions, retention periods and insurance floors are obligations.
