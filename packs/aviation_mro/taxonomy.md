# Obligation classes in an MRO agreement

The family's defining feature: most duties are **conditional on a technical finding** rather than on
a date or a volume. Classes marked **no number** carry no threshold and are invisible to
quantitative coverage.

- **turn_time_commitment** — elapsed days or hours to return a unit or aircraft, usually by check
  type or component class. Supplier. Has a measurement.
- **aog_response_commitment** — response, dispatch or replacement time for an aircraft on ground,
  tiered by severity or station. Distinct from ordinary turn time, with its own clock and premium.
- **labour_rate** — an hourly shop or line rate, often by trade, shift or licence class, with
  escalation.
- **material_handling_markup** — a percentage added to parts cost, sometimes capped per unit or per
  event. A rate on someone else's price.
- **flight_hour_rate** — power-by-the-hour or fixed-per-cycle charge, with a minimum utilisation
  assumption and a reconciliation mechanic.
- **workscope_and_exclusion_rule** — what a quoted price covers and what is billed above it
  (beyond-economic-repair findings, corrosion, foreign object damage, life-limited parts). Governs
  every price record around it. **Usually no number.**
- **findings_and_approval_duty** — the duty to stop, report a finding and obtain written approval
  before exceeding a quoted amount or scope, within a stated window. Supplier. Often has a deadline
  and a money threshold.
- **certification_and_release_duty** — release to service on the required certificate under the
  governing approval. **No number**, and a genuine obligation.
- **airworthiness_and_records_duty** — maintenance records, traceability of parts to origin, dirty
  fingerprint, retention period. Mostly qualitative with a retention period.
- **warranty_obligation** — a period or usage window during which rework is free, with its own
  exclusions and a claim-notice deadline.
- **spares_pool_availability** — a fill-rate or availability percentage for a pooled inventory, and
  the loan or exchange fee when it is missed.
- **reliability_target** — removal rate, unscheduled removal, or mean time between failures, often
  measured per 1,000 flight hours.
- **customer_furnished_obligation** — client duties: supplying parts, records, access, tooling, or
  the aircraft in a stated condition by a stated date. Client. Routinely missed because extraction
  looks for supplier duties.
- **price_escalation_rule** — indexation of labour and material rates, usually on an anniversary
  against a published index with a floor or a cap.
- **liability_cap** · **insurance_floor** · **termination_trigger** · **audit_right** — standard,
  mostly no number beyond a period.
