# Worked shapes

- A turn-time row `C-Check | 21 calendar days from induction` is one record whose threshold is the
  duration and whose trigger is the start event. Losing the start event makes it unmeasurable.
- A material markup ("cost plus twelve percent, capped at a stated amount per unit") is a rate on
  another party's price plus a ceiling — two components of one record, not two records.
- "Findings exceeding the quoted amount require written approval, notified within two business days"
  is a single duty carrying a money threshold and a deadline.
- An exclusion ("corrosion beyond the manual's allowable limits is billed at time and material") is
  an obligation with a consequence, not commentary on the price above it.
- "The Customer shall deliver the aircraft drained, with records in the stated condition" is a client
  obligation. Extraction that looks only for supplier duties loses the entire customer-furnished
  article.
