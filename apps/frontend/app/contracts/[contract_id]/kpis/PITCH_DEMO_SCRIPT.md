# Contract Obligation Tracking — Product Pitch Script

---

### 🟢 PHASE 0: Portfolio Baseline (Pre-Ingestion)
> **UI Location**: Project Dashboard Tab

**🖥️ WHAT TO DO ON SCREEN**:
1. Open the main **Project Dashboard**.
2. Hover over the summary cards (*Total Obligations*, *Compliance Rate*, *Breach Count*, *$ At Risk*).
3. Briefly show the current portfolio graphs.

**🗣️ WHAT TO SPEAK (VERBAL SCRIPT)**:
> *"Good morning everyone. Managing enterprise contracts today is notoriously broken. Millions of dollars in negotiated SLAs and penalty clauses sit hidden inside flat PDF documents, completely unmonitored.*
>
> *Before we add our new agreement, take a look at our live Project Dashboard. This represents our existing enterprise portfolio baseline — tracking historical obligations, exposure, and supplier compliance metrics.*
>
> *Now, let's ingest a complex ground-handling contract — `airport-charges-2025.pdf` — and watch our platform turn legal terms into live operational telemetry."*

---

### 🟢 PHASE 1: Contract Ingestion, Automated KPI Extraction & PDF Traceability
> **UI Location**: Project Upload → Contract View → KPI Compliance Tab

**🖥️ WHAT TO DO ON SCREEN**:
1. Drag and drop `airport-charges-2025.pdf` into the upload zone.
2. Click into the newly ingested contract → select **KPI Compliance** tab.
3. Click the **Extract Obligations** button and wait for completion.
4. Click **Accept All Recommended** to move all 19 KPIs to active status.
5. Click on **KPI `kpi_1`** in the list, then click **Refer Contract**.

**🗣️ WHAT TO SPEAK (VERBAL SCRIPT)**:
> *"I'll upload our airport charges agreement. In seconds, the platform ingests and parses the legal structure.*
>
> *When we open the KPI Compliance workspace and click 'Extract Obligations', our AI compliance engine identifies all contractual obligations — returning 19 structured KPIs across tracked and recommended operational areas.*
>
> *We'll accept all recommended obligations into our active register. But here is the critical feature for legal and audit teams: when I select any KPI and click 'Refer Contract', the platform immediately opens the original legal PDF and highlights the exact clause text from which this KPI was derived. Zero hallucination, 100% legal grounding."*

---

### 🟢 PHASE 2: Multi-Source Data Ingestion & One-Click Smart Matching
> **UI Location**: Sources Panel

**🖥️ WHAT TO DO ON SCREEN**:
1. Click **Sources** in the left navigation sidebar.
2. Point out **Recent Connections** (4 seeded integration profiles) and click **Use All (4)**.
3. Select **Airport Operations CSV** → Click **Upload files** → Click **Smart Match**.
4. Select **Airport Charges JSON** → Click **Upload files** → Click **Smart Match**.
5. Click **Smart Match** on the **REST API Feed** and **SAP S/4HANA** sources.

**🗣️ WHAT TO SPEAK (VERBAL SCRIPT)**:
> *"A contractual obligation is useless without live operational data. In our Sources panel, we connect our enterprise systems.*
>
> *By clicking 'Use All', we populate our ready integration profiles for CSV, JSON, REST APIs, and SAP S/4HANA ERP.*
>
> *Watch how effortless telemetry linking is: we upload an operational CSV log, click 'Smart Match', and the system automatically maps column schemas directly to contractual KPI definitions. We repeat this for our JSON stream, REST API, and SAP feed. Live telemetry is now continuously feeding our contract engine."*

---

### 🟢 PHASE 3: Compliance Breach Detection & Automated Escalation
> **UI Location**: Flags Panel

**🖥️ WHAT TO DO ON SCREEN**:
1. Click **Flags** in the sidebar to reveal active breaches.
2. Expand the **Details** chevron on the top breach (e.g. De-icing / Ground Handling SLA).
3. Walk through the **Metric Detail**, **Expected vs Actual**, **Penalty Exposure**, and **Remedy Text**.
4. Click **Escalate** (Mail icon) → Review the pre-populated Escalation Email modal → Click **Dispatch Alert**.
5. Highlight the status badge changing from **Open (Red)** to **In Action (Blue)**.

**🗣️ WHAT TO SPEAK (VERBAL SCRIPT)**:
> *"As data streams in, our engine automatically detects 6 active contract breaches. Let's inspect this critical Ground Handling SLA breach.*
>
> *The system shows us expected vs actual performance, variance, full remedy clause text, and exact financial penalty exposure calculated in real time.*
>
> *Instead of waiting for quarterly vendor reviews, we click 'Escalate'. The system auto-generates a legal notification with full penalty metrics and contract references. Dispatching the alert logs the communication and instantly transitions the status from 'Open' to 'In Action'."*

### 🟢 PHASE 4: Recoveries Management & Audit Follow-ups
> **UI Location**: Recoveries Tab

**🖥️ WHAT TO DO ON SCREEN**:
1. Click **Recoveries** in the sidebar.
2. Highlight the escalated recovery displaying **In Action** status.
3. Expand details and click **Send Escalation Alert Email** to show the follow-up flow.
4. Show the updated follow-up history log.

**🗣️ WHAT TO SPEAK (VERBAL SCRIPT)**:
> *"Detecting breaches is great, but recovering revenue leakage is where the platform delivers pure bottom-line ROI.*
>
> *In Recoveries Management, financial teams track every dollar subject to penalty claim. The item we just escalated is clearly marked 'In Action'.*
>
> *We can issue formal follow-ups directly from this view, maintaining a complete, audit-ready paper trail until funds are recovered or credited."*
