# SGHA conventions

- **Currency is stated per line, not per agreement.** One schedule can price turnarounds in euro,
  cap liability in dollars, bill an overflight fee in dollars, and express an administration fee as
  a percentage — with the unit given as a symbol in one column and a code in another. Read the
  currency of each amount from its own row. Never carry one row's currency to another.
- Charges are quoted per turnaround, per flight, per hour, per operation, per attempt, per litre,
  per application, or per season. The basis is part of the rate.
- Time targets are measured from operational events — *on-chocks*, *STD*, *off-blocks* — not from
  clock times.
- Quality metrics may be rates per thousand passengers as well as percentages.
- Party vocabulary: the supplier is the *Handler* or *Handling Company*; the client is the *Airline*
  or *Carrier*. The *Station* is the airport the agreement covers. Role words, not names.
- Aircraft are identified by MTOW band, family (A320-family), or category (narrow-body, wide-body,
  regional). The same schedule may use more than one.
