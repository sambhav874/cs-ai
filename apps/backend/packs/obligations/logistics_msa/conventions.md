# Logistics MSA conventions

- Performance is stated **monthly**; targets are percentages to one decimal.
- Credits are commonly `per tenth of a percentage point below <threshold>` — a rate, not a flat
  amount. Preserve the basis.
- Damage and loss rates are stated as a percentage *and* per 10,000 units.
- Party vocabulary: the supplier is styled *Logistics Provider* / *Provider* / *Carrier*; the
  client *Shipper* / *Customer* / *Consignee*. These are role words, not names — the named
  entities are defined in the opening article. **Resolve both from the document.**
- Currency: resolved from the document. This pack states no default.
