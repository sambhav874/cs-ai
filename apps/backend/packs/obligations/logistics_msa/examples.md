# Worked records

Shape decisions, not values. Every value below comes from the document it was read in; a different
contract states different ones. Truncated first under budget — nothing depends on this file.

**One KPI section yields several records.** A section with a `**Target:**` line, a band table and a
`**Penalty Structure:**` paragraph is not one obligation. The target is one record; each band row is
one; each per-unit rate stated in the prose below the table is one; a corrective action plan named
in a consequence cell is one more, with its own deadline and no threshold of its own.

**A rate keeps its basis.** `$3,000 per tenth of a percentage point below 96.5%` is an amount, a
unit of shortfall and a trigger threshold. Recorded as a flat `$3,000` it stops being computable.

**A band table with two key dimensions gives one record per row.** `Shipment Type | Failure Band |
Service Credit` is keyed by both columns; four rows are four records, not one ladder record.

**A cap is its own record, and credit records point at it.** An aggregate ceiling stated in a later
article ("aggregate monthly Service Credits are capped at 18% of monthly management fees", with
named carve-outs) bounds every credit extracted from the KPI article.

**A prohibition is an obligation.** "No detention, demurrage, storage… charge may be billed unless
supported by time-stamped carrier records and approved exception codes" has no threshold, no
percentage and no deadline. It is a client-protective billing precondition owed by the supplier, and
it is exactly the record a number-hunting pass drops.

**Ambiguous ownership stays ambiguous.** Where a duty names no party, `party_role` is null with
`needs_review`. The family does not decide it.
