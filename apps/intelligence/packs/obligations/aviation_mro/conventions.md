# MRO conventions

- Time is measured in flight hours, flight cycles and calendar days, and the same commitment may use
  more than one. Preserve which.
- Turn time is elapsed days from a named start event, not working days, unless the contract says so.
- Rates split into labour (per hour) and material (a percentage on cost). A quoted "price" is often
  a not-to-exceed estimate rather than a fixed sum — the wording distinguishes them.
- Reliability is stated per 1,000 flight hours or as a mean time between events.
- Party vocabulary: the supplier is the *MRO*, *Contractor*, *Service Provider*, or *Repair Station*;
  the client is the *Customer*, *Operator*, or *Owner*. Role words, not names.
- Currency and the index used for escalation are read from the document. This pack states neither.
