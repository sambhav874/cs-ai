# Where SGHA obligations hide

- **The agreement is a set of documents.** A main body, Annex A (scope), Annex B (charges), and rate
  revision notices or amendments. The rate is in a different document from the scope it prices and
  the liability regime that bounds it. A rate record read alone states less than the contract does.
- **An amendment restates only the lines it changes.** "Revised Paragraph 1.2" carries the whole
  band table; a later notice carries the same table again with new numbers. Same obligation, several
  documents, different effective dates — keep the clause reference and the effective date.
- **Annex B rate rows carry an SGHA reference column** (`3.6.1(a)`, `SGHA 2018`, `SGHA Ref`). That
  is the clause reference for the row, not a quantity.
- **The `PRICE` column is not always a number.** `FREE`, `at cost`, `on request (R)` and
  `centralized` are the stated price. Each is still a service the handler owes.
- **Tiered turnaround charges are banded by MTOW or aircraft category**, with a scope description
  in the same row. Band, charge and scope are one record each; the scope column is not decoration.
- **Labour tables are a matrix**: one column per shift class, one row per position. The multipliers
  (1.5x, 2.0x) are part of the rate.
- **Targets and their credits live in different tables.** An SLA table gives metric, target and
  measurement window; a separate table maps a described breach to a credit. Link them; neither is
  complete alone.
- **Pass-through tables carry their own currency column** and the third party doing the billing.
- **Rate escalation sits in its own paragraph** and governs every rate in the schedule.
- **The main body holds liability, insurance, notice, termination and governing law** — and the
  liability cap may be re-banded by a later amendment.
